Privacy Policy for Kalman Katse

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Kalman Katse ("we," "our," or "us") operates a private creator automation service. This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when using our service and the choices you have associated with that data.

1. Information We Collect

Kalman Katse is an internal automation backend used solely to format, render, schedule, and publish video content to connected creator accounts. We do not collect, harvest, sell, or commercialize any end-user personal information.

2. Use of Data and Third-Party APIs

Our service connects to third-party developer APIs (including the TikTok Content Posting API, Meta Graph API, and YouTube Data API) strictly to authenticate uploads and publish original video files to authorized creator profiles.

  • Authentication tokens are securely stored locally and used exclusively to facilitate automated publishing requests.
  • We do not share API tokens or creator account information with any external third parties.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain temporary media files only as long as necessary to complete rendering and scheduled uploads. Users may revoke API access or request deletion of any locally cached credentials at any time by contacting our support address.

4. Children's Privacy

Our service does not address and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: support@kalmankatse.internal
